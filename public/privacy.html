Privacy

Hoodoo, a Bryce-style 3D landscape studio · updated 2026-10-12

Without signing in

Hoodoo runs in your browser. Your scenes, settings and saved skies stay in this browser's own storage and are never sent to us. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Hoodoo Cloud (optional)

Hoodoo Cloud is a public Gallery. Your projects are saved on your device or in your own cloud, not ours. If you sign in with Google, we keep only your Google account ID and your chosen nickname. We check your verified email during sign-in to recognise moderators, but do not store or show your email or name.

We store your public posts and their scene files and pictures, likes, dislikes and reports. Posts show only your nickname. Moderators can hide or delete posts that break the terms; hidden posts are available only to their author and moderators.

IP addresses are kept for rate limits for up to 2 days. Sessions last 30 days; your sign-in cookie is HttpOnly and only its hash is kept on our side. Data lives on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and R2 or KV). Cloudflare Turnstile checks for bots when you publish, vote or report.

Your control

You can change your nickname and delete your public posts in Hoodoo Cloud. Hidden posts are kept for moderator review. “Delete account” in Hoodoo Cloud removes your account, all your posts and files, votes, reports and sessions together, including hidden posts.
