Privacy

Hoodoo, a Bryce-style 3D landscape studio · updated 2026-10-12

Without signing in

Hoodoo runs in your browser. Your scenes, settings and saved skies stay in this browser's own storage and are never sent to us. There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party trackers.

Hoodoo Cloud (optional)

If you sign in with Google to use Hoodoo Cloud, we receive from Google your account ID, email address and name, and nothing else. We use them only to keep your cloud scenes yours and to show your name on posts you publish to the Gallery.

We store: the scenes and pictures you save or publish, your likes, dislikes and reports, and a sign-in cookie (only a hash of it is kept on our side). IP addresses are kept for up to 2 days for rate limits. Sessions last 30 days. Data lives on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 and KV). Cloudflare Turnstile checks for bots when you publish, vote or report.

Published posts are public. Moderators can hide or delete posts that break the terms.

Your control

You can delete any of your cloud scenes and posts in the app at any time. “Delete account” in Hoodoo Cloud deletes your whole account and everything in it at once.
